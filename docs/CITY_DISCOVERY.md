# City-first restaurant discovery

The primary route helps a founder find areas to investigate without supplying candidate addresses, rents or coordinates. The secondary comparison route handles locations already known.

Choose a city, radius, restaurant concept and one to three audience references. Brands resolve before same-name outlets when the exact brand exists. Optional outlet buffers and a disclosed popularity floor alter eligibility. Format, prices and hours guide research only when supplied.

The map displays actual returned grid points, not neighbourhood averages or available properties. Selectable cards and markers stay linked. Changing the brief invalidates old results and research. Location suggestions, explicit city confirmation and direct coordinate entry remain supported where appropriate.

Current ranking and missing-data behavior are defined in SCOUT_AGENT.md. Counts and metrics remain inspectable in the method drawer. A geocoder label can be broad, incomplete or unavailable; it does not establish a property boundary or commercial suitability.
