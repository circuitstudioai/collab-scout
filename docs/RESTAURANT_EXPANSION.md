# Restaurant scouting and expansion

Sitebrief supports independent restaurant operators, small chains, franchise operators and advisers who need a scouting shortlist. This is not a general-business suitability engine or a franchise-management platform. No customer validation or study-cost saving is claimed.

Optional existing outlets and straight-line distance buffers exclude points before selection. The rule is not a cannibalization forecast, drive-time analysis or protected-territory check. A popularity floor and taste screen remain binding; no rule is silently relaxed to fill three cards.

The agent chooses visit order; deterministic code states actual ranks and ties. Cited research helps formulate checks, not approve a unit. For the owner-specified café with an indoor playground, verify usable space, access and permissions without assuming demand from parents or children.

Provider retrieval, selection, structured orchestration and presentation remain separate. Additional industries require their own evidence and rules rather than an unsupported generic dropdown.
