# Sitebrief demo checklist

- Use https://sitebrief-circuitstudio.vercel.app and link https://github.com/circuitstudioai/sitebrief.
- Start **Try Blue Bottle in Toronto**. The proposed expansion is the lead; **Or try the playground café** is secondary.
- Distinguish a fresh live run, a reused real run and a dated saved reference. Show the applicable timestamp/banner.
- Show actual tool activity, selected points, popularity floor, exact rank and ties. The server locks the first visit; do not describe taste as demand or a market gap.
- Show the Qloo audience references and explain that nearby venues are a ranked sample, not a competitor census.
- Show all four local summaries withheld. Open a source link as inspection material, not proof of proximity, availability or suitability.
- Show the fixed City/landlord questions as checks to perform, not legal conclusions.
- Download the real one-page PDF and scouting notes; both preserve point identity, limits and saved/cached labels.
- Keep credentials and private account pages out of recordings.
- Shared Redis admission is active at 50 live jobs per UTC day per environment. Per-visitor limits and MCP authentication are not deployed; do not claim them.
- Confirm final recording and screenshots show the renamed URL and released source-only behavior before using them in Devpost.
- Recording, filing and submission receipts are separate actions; this checklist does not claim they are complete.
